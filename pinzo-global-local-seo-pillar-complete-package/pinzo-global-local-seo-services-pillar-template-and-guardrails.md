### Keyword placement rules

1. Use `local SEO services` in the title, H1, first paragraph, one H2, service definition, pricing section, FAQ and conclusion.
2. Use singular and semantic variants only where they read naturally.
3. Do not repeat `local SEO company` or `local SEO agency` in every section.
4. Do not claim that Pinzo is the best Local SEO company unless a dated, independent and verifiable award supports the statement.
5. Do not target `near me` as a Pinzo office claim. Pinzo provides services globally and must not imply a physical office in each searcher's city.
6. Keep Google Business Profile language present but secondary. This page covers the entire Local SEO system.
7. Use AI search language with clear limits. Pinzo can monitor and improve supporting signals. Pinzo cannot control third-party answers.

## SERP and competitor findings

Research completed on 2026-07-25.

### Competitor patterns worth matching

| Competitor | Strong pattern | Pinzo response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WebFX | Broad service list, pricing estimator, case studies, deliverable table and detailed FAQs | Full 12-part service matrix, pricing factors, selected work, monthly deliverables and 12 FAQs |
| BrightLocal | Simple managed-service proposition, clear 90-day process, visible public price and service pillars | Platform, managed and hybrid models with a specific 90-day roadmap |
| Thrive | Wide keyword coverage, multi-location content, buyer guide and AI visibility language | Natural keyword coverage, branch workflows, provider checklist and defensible AI wording |
| Searchbloom | Strong Local SEO explanation, citations, content, reviews and proof | Direct answer, connected search journey and a broader website plus Maps plus AI system |

### Gaps the Pinzo page should outperform

1. Most competitor pages are agency-only. Pinzo combines an operating platform with managed execution.
2. Many pages treat Google Business Profile work as the whole of Local SEO. Pinzo separates profile management from website, citations, reviews, authority, technical SEO and conversions.
3. Multi-location content is often generic. Pinzo explains branch governance, role-based access, leaderboards, exception reporting and local-page quality.
4. AI visibility claims are often vague. Pinzo names the measurable workflow: prompts, mentions, cited sources, factual accuracy and recommended fixes.
5. Few competitors explain how a lead form is verified. Pinzo requires confirmed delivery before a success message appears.
6. Few pages explain doorway-page and scaled-content risk. Pinzo documents a noindex and quality gate for future country, city and industry pages.
7. Pricing pages often blend software and service fees. Pinzo separates platform subscription, recurring management and one-time remediation.

## Content voice and anti-AI guardrails

1. Do not use en dash or em dash characters.
2. Do not use named HTML entities for long dash characters.
3. Use commas, colons, periods and parentheses where needed.
4. Write in direct business language.
5. Avoid filler openings such as `In today's digital landscape`.
6. Avoid repetitive structures such as `It is not just X, it is Y`.
7. Avoid three consecutive sentences with the same length or opening.
8. Use specific nouns: calls, bookings, direction requests, reviews, locations and service pages.
9. Explain every acronym on first use except SEO.
10. Do not claim that AI systems use a single published ranking formula.
11. Do not say that reviews, posts or citations alone guarantee rankings.
12. Do not write location pages by swapping city names.
13. Do not invent market statistics.
14. Do not invent case-study metrics.
15. Do not call Pinzo award-winning without evidence.
16. Do not claim Google partnership or endorsement.
17. Use `Google Business Profile`, with `Google My Business` only where needed as a legacy search synonym.

- Exactly one H1.
- Self-canonical with a trailing slash.
- `x-default` only until live localized alternates exist.
- All navigation anchors must match an element ID.
- New-tab links must include `rel="noopener"`.
- No duplicate HTML IDs.
- No inline personal data in analytics events.
- Mobile navigation must be keyboard accessible.
- Tables must remain horizontally scrollable on small screens.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Maintain visible focus states.
- Do not hide primary content behind JavaScript.
- Keep a working HTML form action as a non-JavaScript fallback.
- Add an approved Open Graph image when a crawlable image is available.

## Indexation and scaled-content guardrails

The Global Pillar can be indexed after final QA because it is one unique authority page.

Future country, city and combination pages remain `noindex,follow` until:

- The page has a unique search intent.
- Country, city or industry context is useful and accurate.
- Proof is approved or clearly labelled as a sample.
- Internal links are live.
- Canonical and hreflang are valid.
- The form delivers the lead.
- Schema matches visible content.
- No copied locality blocks are present.
- Quality score is at least 80.

Pages created mainly to rank for similar location combinations without useful differences must not be published.
