## Search strategy

### Primary intent

The page targets businesses comparing local SEO services, agencies, companies, consultants and experts in Mumbai. It must answer what is included, how Mumbai catchments affect visibility, how the work differs for one location and many locations, how performance is measured, what it costs and how long progress can take.

### Keyword map

| Placement | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Title, H1, opening answer, FAQ heading | local SEO services in Mumbai |
| Meta description and body | Google Maps visibility, local rankings, reviews, local leads |
| Supporting body copy | local SEO agency in Mumbai |
| Supporting body copy and FAQ | local SEO company in Mumbai |
| Selection section | local SEO consultant Mumbai, local SEO expert Mumbai |
| Service and FAQ sections | Google Business Profile optimisation, local search audit, local citation building, review management, location page SEO |

Use exact phrases only where they read naturally. Do not insert every variation into every section. Keep Mumbai intent on the City Pillar and send industry intent to specialist child pages.

## Content guardrails

### Required

* Write a city strategy, not a city name replacement.
* Explain real neighbourhood, travel and catchment logic.
* Separate Maps visibility from organic website visibility.
* Cover Google Business Profile, website SEO, reviews, citations, technical SEO, authority, tracking and reporting.
* Explain single location and multi location differences.
* Include at least one direct answer near the top.
* Include ten to twelve useful FAQs that match visible page copy and schema.
* Add clear internal links to the country parent, global parent, GBP service and live industry child pages.
* Label illustrative screenshots and replace them with verified assets before indexation.
* Use qualified language for results. Visibility can improve, but rankings cannot be guaranteed.
* Keep every image alt attribute descriptive and specific to its purpose.
* Verify the lead is received before publishing or running paid traffic.

### Prohibited

* No en dash or em dash characters.
* No fake statistics, customer counts, awards, reviews or case studies.
* No best agency claim without independent evidence.
* No guarantee of first position, three pack placement, lead volume or timeline.
* No fake office, virtual office, keyword stuffed business name or review gating advice.
* No cloned location pages that only swap place names.
* No links to unpublished child pages.
* No FAQ schema for questions that are not visible on the page.
* No LocalBusiness address schema for Pinzo unless the page represents a real eligible Pinzo location in Mumbai.
* No unsupported prices in metadata or copy.
* No silent JavaScript success state when the form has not delivered data.

## Human writing rules

Use concrete business language and varied sentence lengths. Prefer examples that clarify decisions. Avoid vague phrases such as unlock growth, dominate your market, game changer, cutting edge and skyrocket. Do not force synonyms simply to increase keyword coverage. Every section must add a new decision, fact, comparison, workflow or risk.

Do not begin alt text with image of or picture of. Leave decorative images with an empty alt attribute. Do not place target keywords in every image.

## Internal linking rules

The City Pillar links upward to the India Country Pillar and Global Pillar. It links sideways to Google Business Profile management and relevant resources. It links downward to live Industry by City pages. Each child page should link back to this City Pillar with a natural anchor such as local SEO services across Mumbai.

Minimum contextual links: 12. Do not create a context free link wall. Check that every destination returns a successful response before indexation.

## Schema rules

Use `Service`, `FAQPage` and `BreadcrumbList` when the visible content supports them. Keep service area information truthful. Do not add ratings or reviews to schema without valid first party evidence. Keep schema URLs canonical and absolute.

## Indexation gate

Publish as `noindex,follow` until all items pass:

* Unique city research is complete.
* Title, description, H1 and canonical are unique.
* City catchments and search behaviour are original.
* Proof assets are verified and not placeholders.
* At least 12 internal links are live and relevant.
* FAQ copy and FAQ schema match.
* Form delivery is tested end to end.
* Mobile layout, accessibility and Core Web Vitals are acceptable.
* No en dash or em dash characters remain.
* No unsupported claim remains.
* Content quality score is at least 85.

## QA checklist

1. Search the HTML, CSV and Markdown for Unicode U+2013 and U+2014.
2. Confirm title is concise and includes the primary term.
3. Confirm meta description states the service, city and outcome without a guarantee.
4. Confirm one H1 only.
5. Confirm heading order is logical.
6. Confirm every meaningful image has an accurate alt attribute.
7. Confirm canonical and hreflang URLs.
8. Validate JSON LD.
9. Test every internal link.
10. Submit a real test lead and verify receipt.
11. Test FAQ controls with keyboard navigation.
12. Test at desktop, tablet and mobile widths.
