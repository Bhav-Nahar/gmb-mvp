Clinic owners, doctors, healthcare marketers, or clinic administrators in Mumbai looking for someone or a system to manage and improve their Google Business Profile.

## City inputs

- City: Mumbai
- Neighborhood examples: Andheri, Borivali, Malad, Bandra, Powai, Kandivali, Dadar, Ghatkopar

## Industry inputs

- Industry: Doctors & Clinics
- Decision factors: reviews, doctor credibility, service clarity, clinic photos, staff behavior, appointment availability, location convenience, timings

  - Review replies are missing or robotic
  - Photos do not show clinic trust

  - No weekly updates/posts
  - No visibility into calls, direction requests, or profile actions
- Category examples:
  - Medical Clinic

  - Eye Care Center if eye clinic
  - Fertility Clinic if fertility clinic
- Service examples:
  - Consultation
  - Health checkup
  - Diagnosis-specific consultation
  - Follow-up consultation
  - Preventive care
  - Specialist treatment pages, only if actually offered

- Do not guarantee medical outcomes.
- Do not use fake reviews or review incentives.
- Escalate sensitive complaints to the clinic team.
