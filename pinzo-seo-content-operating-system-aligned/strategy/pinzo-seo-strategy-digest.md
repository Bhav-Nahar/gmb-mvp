# Pinzo SEO Strategy Alignment Digest

This file is the strategy source of truth extracted from `pinzo_comprehensive_seo_strategy_roadmap.xlsx`. The skill must use these rules before generating any Pinzo website content.

## Strategic thesis

| Section | Strategy Detail | Execution Rule | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strategic Thesis | Pinzo should build the category around Google Business Profile operations, not only generic 'GMB tool' keywords. | Create one product authority hub and multiple problem/feature/industry clusters. | This captures both high-volume educational traffic and high-intent buyer traffic. |
| Keyword Reality | The uploaded universe is large, but most volume is navigational/head-term demand with high difficulty. | Use head terms for homepage relevance; use long-tail pain queries for first traffic wins. | This avoids wasting 6 months chasing keywords Google/large sites already own. |
| Topical Authority | Build an interconnected GBP knowledge base: setup, verification, optimization, reviews, posts, photos, services, categories, suspension, reporting. | Every page must link to 3–8 related pages and one conversion asset. | Google needs to understand Pinzo as a specialist, not a random SaaS website. |
| Product-led SEO | Free tools should become the traffic-to-lead bridge: audit, description generator, category finder, review reply generator, post ideas. | Launch tools as indexable pages with examples and FAQs. | Utility pages earn links and convert better than plain articles. |
| BOFU Foundation | Publish product, service, feature, agency, and industry pages before scaling blogs. | SEO traffic without BOFU pages leaks conversion. | A SaaS/managed service needs sales pages to convert organic demand. |
| Industry Wedge | Start with doctors/clinics because Pinzo already has healthcare traction, then expand to jewellery, restaurants, salons, coaching, retail chains. | Each industry page should include specific categories, services, reviews, post ideas, and reporting examples. | Industry pages turn generic GBP features into business pain/value. |
| Programmatic SEO | Use pSEO only after core pages index and after a quality template is proven. | Start with 25 pages max, evaluate indexation and impressions, then scale. | Prevents thin-page risk while unlocking thousands of long-tail combinations. |
| CRO Layer | Every SEO page needs a next step based on intent: audit, checklist, generator, consultation, report sample, or trial. | Do not use the same CTA everywhere. | Intent-matched CTAs improve lead rate and lead quality. |
| Content Quality | Pages must answer fully, include examples, templates, screenshots/tool outputs, and cite official Google guidance where relevant. | Avoid generic AI copy and keyword stuffing. | Google’s people-first content guidance rewards useful, original content. |
| Measurement | Segment Search Console by /guides/, /features/, /industries/, /tools/ and track assisted conversions. | Review weekly in first 90 days, then monthly. | Traffic only matters if it creates demos, trials, or managed-service leads. |

## Non-negotiable alignment rules

1. Use the Page Roadmap URL mapping as the primary canonical source. Do not create a new URL when the keyword already maps to an existing roadmap URL.
2. Build P1 pages before scaling P2/P3 blog volume. P1 pages are conversion infrastructure and topical-authority anchors.
3. Head terms such as `google my business` must support the product authority hub, not standalone thin articles.
4. Every page must have a CTA and lead magnet matched to the entry intent.
5. Every guide must link to at least one conversion page and at least two related educational pages.
6. Programmatic SEO must not start at scale until core pages index and prove impressions. Start with 25 pages max where the workbook says so.
7. Support/contact pages must clearly state Pinzo is not Google and must point users to official support routes.
8. Troubleshooting pages must avoid guarantees, impersonation, black-hat tactics, fake reviews, or unsafe review-removal promises.
9. Industry pages must include industry-specific categories, services, review examples, post examples, photo examples, and pain points. Never swap only the industry name.
10. Tool pages must include explainer content, examples, limitations, FAQs, schema, and a next step. A tool-only page is not publish-ready.
