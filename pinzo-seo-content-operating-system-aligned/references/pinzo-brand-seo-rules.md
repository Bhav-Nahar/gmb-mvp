# Pinzo Brand and SEO Safety Rules

## Positioning

Pinzo is an AI-powered Google Business Profile management tool and managed service.

Safe descriptions:

- Pinzo helps businesses manage and improve their Google Business Profile.
- Pinzo helps agencies and brands monitor reviews, posts, profile quality, and local visibility.
- Pinzo can support managed GBP workflows for clinics, local businesses, agencies, and multi-location brands.
- Pinzo is being built as a local growth operating system around Google Business Profile.

## Do not claim

- Pinzo is Google.
- Pinzo is official Google support.
- Pinzo guarantees rankings.
- Pinzo guarantees verification.
- Pinzo guarantees review removal.
- Pinzo can remove any bad review.
- Pinzo can bypass Google's policies.
- Planned modules are live unless confirmed.

## Required disclaimers by page type

### Support / customer care pages

State clearly:

- Pinzo is not Google.
- Pinzo cannot replace official Google Business Profile support.
- For official help, use Google Business Profile Help.
- Pinzo can help you understand the issue, prepare your profile, and manage GBP operations.

### Troubleshooting / suspension pages

State clearly:

- Reinstatement depends on Google's review.
- Documentation and profile accuracy matter.
- Do not create duplicate profiles or misrepresent business information.
- No guaranteed reinstatement.

### Review pages

State clearly:

- Do not buy, gate, or fake reviews.
- Only flag reviews that appear to violate Google policies.
- Reply professionally and document issues.
- Pinzo can help with review workflows and response quality.

## Tone

Use a founder-led, practical, expert tone. Avoid inflated enterprise SaaS jargon.

Good tone:

- "Here is the exact workflow..."
- "Use this checklist before you publish..."
- "For a clinic, this means..."
- "Pinzo can help you operationalize this across locations."

Bad tone:

- "Revolutionary AI-powered solution..."
- "Guaranteed top ranking..."
- "Remove all negative reviews instantly..."
- "Official Google support number..."
