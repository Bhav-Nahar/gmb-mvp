# Pinzo AIO / GEO / AEO Framework

Every Pinzo cornerstone page must be answer-engine friendly.

## AIO requirements

- Direct answer block in first 150 words.
- Definitions for Google Business Profile terms.
- Clear steps for how-to queries.
- Tables for decisions and comparisons.
- FAQ section with concise answers.
- Summary section that can be quoted by AI answers.
- Source notes to official Google docs when discussing processes.

## GEO requirements

- Include entity-rich context around Google Business Profile, Google Maps, local pack, reviews, posts, categories, services, verification, and insights.
- Use consistent Pinzo entity names: free GBP audit, listing quality score, AI review replies, Google Posts scheduler, Services & Category Finder, white-label reports.
- Include original frameworks such as scorecards, workflows, checklists, and templates.
- Include use-case examples for target industries.

## AEO requirements

- Answer questions directly.
- Use question-format H2/H3s where natural.
- Include voice-search friendly definitions.
- Provide "what to do next" steps.
- Keep answers accurate and non-misleading.

## Mandatory blocks

For every strategic page include:

1. "Quick answer" or "TL;DR".
2. "When you should use this".
3. "Step-by-step workflow" or "Checklist".
4. "Common mistakes".
5. "How Pinzo helps".
6. "FAQs".
7. "Next step CTA".
