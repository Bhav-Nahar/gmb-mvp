# Pinzo Programmatic SEO Publishing Workflow

Programmatic SEO is a growth lever, not a shortcut.

## Allowed pSEO templates

| Template | URL Pattern | CTA | Rollout |
|---|---|---|---|
| Industry × City | `/google-business-profile-management-for-{industry}-in-{city}/` | Book local GBP audit | Start with 25 pages: 5 industries × 5 cities |
| Industry post examples | `/google-business-profile-post-ideas-for-{industry}/` | Generate 30 posts | Priority industries first |
| Industry review replies | `/google-review-reply-examples-for-{industry}/` | Try AI review replies | Doctors, restaurants, salons, jewellery first |
| Service extraction | `/google-business-profile-services-for-{industry}/` | Generate missing services | After services module is live |
| Category selection | `/best-google-business-profile-category-for-{industry}/` | Find right category | Start with 20 industries |
| Suspension reasons | `/google-business-profile-suspended-{reason}/` | Suspension checklist | Accuracy reviewed only |
| Competitor alternatives | `/alternatives/{competitor}/` | Book demo | After product proof |
| City agency | `/google-business-profile-management-agency-in-{city}/` | WhatsApp consultation | Active sales cities only |

## Mandatory uniqueness fields

Every pSEO page must have:

- Unique intro.
- Unique industry/city context.
- Unique examples.
- Unique services/categories.
- Unique Google Posts ideas or review examples where relevant.
- Unique FAQs.
- Unique internal links.
- A real conversion CTA.
- Human QA.

## Doorway page prevention

Do not publish if:

- Only the city or industry name changes.
- No local or vertical detail exists.
- There is no unique example.
- It does not link to the parent hub.
- It targets a location where Pinzo has no active sales or service plan.
- It cannot satisfy intent without the user clicking another page.

## Rollout gate

Before scaling:

1. Parent hub is live.
2. Sitemap submitted.
3. Initial pages indexed.
4. GSC impressions visible.
5. At least one conversion signal tracked.
6. Thin-page and duplicate checks passed.
