# Pinzo Cornerstone Content Framework

A Pinzo cornerstone page is not a blog post. It is an authority asset, conversion page, and internal-linking hub.

## Cornerstone page must include

1. Strategy-mapped URL.
2. Clear primary keyword and intent.
3. Direct answer block within the first 150 words.
4. Deep H2/H3 structure.
5. Practical examples.
6. Workflow, checklist, template, scorecard, or tool section.
7. Pinzo-specific moat.
8. Internal links to feature, service, guide, industry, and tool pages.
9. Intent-matched CTA and lead magnet.
10. FAQ block.
11. Schema recommendation.
12. Image/screenshot brief.
13. CMS-ready metadata.
14. Publish-readiness score.

## Pinzo-specific cornerstone formats

### Product authority hub

Used for `/google-business-profile-management-tool/`.

Must include:

- Business pain.
- Pinzo promise.
- Dashboard/workflow sections.
- Review management.
- Posts scheduler.
- Profile audit.
- Geo-grid / rank tracking if live or clearly marked planned.
- Reporting.
- Agency and multi-location use cases.
- Pricing path.
- Free GBP audit.
- FAQs.

### Optimization pillar

Used for `/guides/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist/`.

Must include:

- 100-point GBP score framework.
- NAP, categories, services, description, photos, reviews, posts, Q&A, products, hours, appointment links, and tracking.
- Mistakes.
- Checklist.
- Free GBP audit CTA.
- Links to categories, services, review management, posts scheduler, rank tracker.

### Verification pillar

Used for `/guides/google-business-profile-verification/`.

Must include:

- Verification methods.
- Postcard, phone, email, video, and bulk verification notes.
- Delays and code issues.
- What not to do.
- Readiness checklist.
- Official support route notes.
- Managed setup CTA.

### Review management pillar

Used for `/features/google-review-management/` and review guides.

Must include:

- Unified inbox.
- AI replies.
- Sentiment / status tags.
- Negative review workflow.
- Policy-safe review request guidance.
- Reply examples.
- CTA to AI Review Reply Generator.

### Industry cornerstone

Used for doctors/clinics and other priority industries.

Must include:

- Industry-specific discovery behavior.
- GBP services and category examples.
- Reviews and reply examples.
- Posts and offer examples.
- Photo and trust proof recommendations.
- Calls, directions, appointment links, lead flow.
- Reporting and governance.
- Audit/demo CTA.

## Thin content rejection criteria

Reject or revise content if:

- It has generic intro copy.
- It only defines the topic without workflows.
- It has no examples or templates.
- It has no internal links.
- It has no CTA.
- It does not match the roadmap.
- It creates a new page that should be part of an existing hub.
- It claims Pinzo features are live without confirmation.
