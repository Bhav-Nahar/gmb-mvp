# Pinzo Page Templates

Use the page type from the roadmap when available.

| Page Type | Recommended Word Count | Required Sections | SERP Differentiator | CTA Blocks | Schema | Quality Bar |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product Landing | 1,500–2,500 | Pain; product promise; feature modules; workflows; screenshots; use cases; integrations; pricing; FAQs | Real dashboard screenshots + free tool + comparison vs manual workflow | Hero, mid-page, exit CTA | SoftwareApplication, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList | Must prove product exists and solve operational pain, not generic SaaS copy. |
| Service Landing | 1,800–2,800 | Pain; deliverables; process; pricing; proof; timelines; sample report; FAQs | Show what Pinzo does weekly/monthly and how success is measured | Book consultation, WhatsApp, audit | Service, FAQPage | Must feel like a sales page with clear package and trust proof. |
| How-To Guide | 1,500–2,500 | Definition; step-by-step; screenshots; mistakes; FAQs; related tools; internal links | Checklist or mini tool + updated official terminology | Free audit/checklist/tool | HowTo, FAQPage | Must answer the task fully before pitching Pinzo. |
| Troubleshooting Guide | 1,800–3,000 | Symptoms; causes; diagnosis; official-safe steps; what not to do; prevention; FAQs | Flowchart/checklist + honest risk notes | Consultation/checklist/review analysis | Article, FAQPage | No guarantees; no impersonation; no unsafe review tactics. |
| Industry Landing | 1,800–3,000 | Industry pain; GBP setup; categories/services; reviews; posts; photos; reporting; case study; pricing | Industry-specific examples and templates | Book audit/demo | Service, FAQPage | Must not be a swapped-industry template; include unique services and local buyer behavior. |
| Tool Page | 800–1,500 plus tool | Tool; how it works; examples; limitations; FAQs; next steps | Interactive utility, saved result, email/WhatsApp capture | Use tool, save report, start trial | SoftwareApplication or WebApplication, FAQPage | Tool must be useful even without signup; content supports ranking. |
| Comparison Page | 1,500–2,500 | Who each is for; feature matrix; pricing caveat; use cases; migration; FAQs | Honest recommendation matrix | Book demo | FAQPage | Fair and factual; updated quarterly. |

## Product Landing Template

Recommended for `/google-business-profile-management-tool/`.

Sections:

1. Hero: category promise + free GBP audit CTA.
2. Pain: why GBP operations fail manually.
3. Product workflow: connect, audit, improve, publish, review, report.
4. Feature modules: reviews, posts, audit score, services, description, competitors, geo-grid, reporting.
5. Use cases: agency, doctors/clinics, multi-location brands, local business.
6. Screenshots/tool-output placeholders.
7. Manual vs Pinzo workflow table.
8. Pricing/demo path.
9. FAQs.
10. Internal links to feature and industry pages.
