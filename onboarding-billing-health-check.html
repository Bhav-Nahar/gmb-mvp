Already found and fixed

From the security review. These were real, and they're closed.

Private data was leaking to people who hadn't paid

Fixed

What it was: a user who hadn't started their trial could still pull premium info (like health scores) if they poked at the app directly.

Now: locked down on the server, not just hidden on screen.

People could get charged twice

Fixed

What it was: clicking "pay" twice, or in two tabs, could quietly set up two separate charges.

Now: only one can happen — the second attempt is blocked and any leftover is cancelled.

The "one free trial per person" rule was easy to cheat

Fixed

What it was: typing the same phone number in a different format (spaces, dashes, +91) fooled the system into granting another free trial.

Now: every format is treated as the same number.

A technical replay trick could refill free credits

Fixed

What it was: a loophole in how we confirm payments could be replayed to top up AI credits for free.

Now: the confirmation is tied to signed data that can't be replayed.

Team members hit a dead button

Fixed

What it was: a non-owner teammate saw "Start Free Trial" but it wouldn't work for them.

Now: they see "ask your admin to start the trial" instead.

A background job could lock a paying customer

Fixed

What it was: a rare timing bug could push a customer who just paid into a "past due" state.

Now: it double-checks before ever downgrading anyone.

Two things you asked for

Done

Fewer steps: phone number and payment are now one screen, one click to Razorpay.

The ₹5 charge: Razorpay fixes this at ₹5 for subscriptions (can't be made ₹1 or ₹0), and it's auto-refunded. The on-screen text now correctly says ₹5.

Before launch

What it is: your customers are Indian businesses. They need a proper GST invoice (with their GST number) to claim the tax back. Right now some payments don't produce one.

Why it matters: without it you look unprofessional and lose deals with serious buyers.
