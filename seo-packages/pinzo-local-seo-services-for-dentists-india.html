AI Search Optimization

How Pinzo supports AI search visibility for dental clinics

Entity consistency

Structured answers

Answer real patient questions clearly

Citation readiness

Strengthen evidence that search systems can retrieve

Build useful pages, relevant third-party mentions, quality reviews, local authority and trustworthy references around the clinic and its practitioners.

Visibility testing
