## 1. Purpose of this page family

The Industry Pillar is the country-adapted parent for one industry. It explains how Local SEO works for that industry across the country, covers the complete service model and routes visitors and internal authority to relevant city child pages.

This page is not:

- A global Local SEO pillar
- A country pillar for every industry
- A city landing page
- An industry and city combination page
- A specialist service page such as Google Maps SEO
- A thin collection of city links

The page must work as a useful standalone guide for the industry while supporting the wider Pinzo Local SEO services universe.

## 9. Human writing guardrails

### Absolute dash rule

The following Unicode characters are prohibited in every generated field and in the HTML:

```text
En dash: U+2013
Em dash: U+2014
```

Use:

- A full stop for a new sentence
- A comma for a short pause
- A colon before an explanation
- Parentheses only when the detail is genuinely secondary
- The word "to" for ranges, such as days 1 to 30
- A normal ASCII hyphen only inside established compound terms such as multi-location, treatment-led or 90-day

Run an automated Unicode scan before export.

### Sentence style

- Mix short and medium sentence lengths.
- Prefer concrete verbs such as audit, fix, map, track, compare, publish and measure.
- Explain the buyer's actual decision before describing Pinzo.
- Use specific industry examples.
- Avoid repeating the same three-part sentence rhythm.
- Avoid empty transitions such as in today's digital landscape, moreover, furthermore and in conclusion.
- Avoid filler such as unlock, elevate, revolutionise, seamless, cutting-edge, game-changing and robust solution.
- Avoid generic fear statements.
- Do not start multiple sections with the same phrase.
- Do not repeat "Pinzo helps" in every paragraph.
- Do not use a city name merely to make generic copy appear local.

### Claims

- Never guarantee rankings, leads, appointments, revenue or AI mentions.
- Do not claim results without a source and client approval.
- Do not invent years of experience, client counts, review counts or case studies.
- Do not call a sample report a client report.
- Do not use unsupported statistics from competitors.
- Explain proximity limits honestly.

### Healthcare language

- Use patients rather than customers for dental and clinical pages.
- Avoid medical outcome promises.
- Keep review replies privacy-conscious.
- Do not request or expose treatment details in public review replies.
- Do not offer diagnosis or treatment advice.
