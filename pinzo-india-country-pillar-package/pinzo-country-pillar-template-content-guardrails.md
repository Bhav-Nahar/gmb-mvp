# Pinzo Country Pillar Template and Content Guardrails

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the production template for a Pinzo Country Pillar in the Local SEO Services universe. The India page at https://www.pinzo.io/en-in/local-seo-services/ is the benchmark implementation.

The Country Pillar must do five jobs:

1. Target national commercial intent for Local SEO services.
2. Explain how search behaviour, terminology, cities and operating conditions differ in the country.
3. Route users and search engines into city, industry and industry by city child pages.
4. Convert single-location businesses, multi-location brands and agencies.
5. Act as the controlled parent for scalable page production without becoming a thin programmatic page.

| Element | Rule |
| --- | --- |

## 4. Heading and Body Coverage

Use one H1 only. The H1 must state the country and core service without extra slogans.

Required H2 topics:

1. Direct answer defining Local SEO services in the country.
2. Country-specific local search behaviour.
3. Complete service scope.
4. Local ranking principles and realistic limitations.
5. City-by-city strategy.
6. Industry-specific strategy.
7. Single-location and multi-location approaches.
8. AI search visibility.
9. Operational safeguards.
10. Monthly deliverables.
11. First 90-day plan.
12. Buyer evaluation criteria.
13. Packages and pricing.
14. Proof and reporting.
15. FAQs.
16. Audit or proposal CTA.

Body content rules:

- Write for the business owner first. Use keywords only when they fit the sentence.
- Use the primary phrase in the first 150 words, the direct answer and one FAQ.
- Give each secondary keyword a distinct job. Do not stack variants in one sentence.
- Explain country differences through search behaviour, city structure, languages where relevant, customer journeys, regulation, branch operations and proof expectations.
- Never produce a country page by changing only the country name.
- Avoid long introductions. Each section should answer one practical question.
- Use examples that could be verified. Do not invent market shares, rankings, customer counts or performance improvements.
- Use Indian English for en-IN pages. Keep spelling and terms consistent.

## 5. Human Writing Guardrails

The page must not read like generated filler.

- Do not use an en dash or em dash. Use a full stop, comma, colon, semicolon or parentheses.
- Do not replace every dash with the same punctuation. Rewrite the sentence so it sounds natural.
- Avoid stock phrases such as unlock growth, elevate your presence, ever-evolving landscape, seamless solution, game changer, tailored strategy, digital age and transform your business.
- Avoid three consecutive adjectives and vague claims such as powerful, comprehensive and cutting-edge.
- Mix sentence length. Prefer concrete nouns and direct verbs.
- Do not begin multiple sections with the same pattern.
- Use contractions only where they sound natural for the brand.
- Do not force exact-match keywords into grammar that a person would not use.
- Read the page aloud before launch. Rewrite any sentence that sounds like a template.

Automated character check:

- Forbidden Unicode characters: U+2013, U+2014, U+2011 and U+2212.
- ASCII hyphens are allowed in normal compounds such as multi-location, on-page and third-party.

Every Country Pillar must cover:

1. Google Business Profile management.

Each row must explain what Pinzo manages and why it matters in that country. Do not copy a generic benefits column across countries.

## 8. City and Industry Hub Rules

- Link to at least six approved city hubs and six approved industry hubs when available.
- Use descriptive anchor text. Never display a bare URL as the main anchor.
- Do not link to a planned page that returns an error.
- A child page must link back to the Country Pillar.
- Country pages should also link to the Global Pillar, GBP management, relevant Pinzo features and pricing.
- Keep at least 15 useful internal links, excluding navigation and footer boilerplate.
- Do not publish city pages for places where the business model has no genuine content or commercial case.

## 9. Image and Alt Attribute Rules

- Every img element must have an alt attribute.
- Describe the image first. Add a keyword only if it accurately describes the image.
- Good: Sample Pinzo Local SEO report for India businesses.
- Bad: Best Local SEO Services India Local SEO Company India.
- Decorative visuals should use an empty alt or aria-hidden.
- CSS-built dashboards should use role="img" and an accurate aria-label.
- Proof visuals must be labelled Sample, Example or Client result as applicable.
- Do not use a client logo, review or screenshot without approval.

## 10. FAQ Rules

- Use 8 to 12 visible FAQs based on real commercial, process, pricing and risk questions.
- The visible FAQ and FAQ schema must use the same question and answer text.
- Include the primary keyword in one FAQ, not every FAQ.
- Cover single location, multi-location, timeline, package scope, pricing factors, agency selection and AI search claims.
- Give direct answers. Avoid sales copy inside answers.
- Never guarantee rankings, lead volume, review growth or AI mentions.

- Do not add Review, AggregateRating or LocalBusiness schema without eligible, visible and verified evidence.
- Do not add fake pricing through Offer. Link to current pricing unless a reviewed public price is displayed.

## 13. Proof and Claim Rules

- Label mock dashboards and reports as illustrative or sample.
- Replace samples with approved evidence before indexation when possible.
- Keep the claim limited to what the evidence proves.
- Do not claim best, top, number one, guaranteed, instant or fixed-percentage growth without dated, verifiable support.
- Explain that Google local visibility is influenced by relevance, distance and prominence.
- State clearly that no provider can guarantee AI citations or recommendations.

## 14. Import Field Schema

The ready-to-upload CSV uses one row per country page. Structured multi-item fields use:

- Double pipe between FAQ items.
- Double colon between a label and its value.
- Single pipe between list items.
- Semicolon between the items of a sub-list nested inside one double-colon part.

Multi-part cells must carry every column the reference HTML shows. A service_matrix
row is `area::what Pinzo manages::why it matters in the country`, a buyer_checklist
row is `ask for::good evidence::warning sign`, a city or industry hub is
`anchor::url::descriptor`, and a packages row is
`tag::name::lead-in::feature;feature;feature::CTA label::CTA URL`. Flattening any of
these into two parts leaves the rendered table or card half empty.

## 15. Prelaunch QA Checklist

1. No en dash, em dash, nonbreaking hyphen or mathematical minus characters.
2. One H1 and a logical H2/H3 structure.
3. Primary keyword appears in title, description, H1, direct answer and one FAQ.
4. Secondary keywords appear only in relevant sections.
5. Title and description lengths are within practical targets.
6. Every image has an alt attribute.
7. CSS visuals have accessible labels.
8. At least 15 contextual internal links are valid.
9. Canonical, locale and hreflang are correct.
10. JSON-LD parses and visible FAQs match.
11. No unsupported ranking, result or AI recommendation claims.
12. Proof is real or clearly labelled as sample.
13. Form submits to a tested destination.
14. Consent, UTM, GCLID and landing-page capture work.
15. Mobile navigation, FAQ accordions and mobile CTA work.
16. Page remains noindex until links, proof and form delivery pass.
17. Run a final human editorial read for repeated phrases and template language.

## 16. India Launch Blockers

Before changing the India page to index,follow:

1. Confirm the Global Pillar is live.
2. Confirm every linked city and industry page returns 200.
3. Replace or approve the labelled sample proof block.
4. Send a test lead and confirm it is stored or received.
5. Add production spam protection and server-side validation.
6. Validate the rendered page on mobile and desktop.
7. Validate schema with the final production URL.
